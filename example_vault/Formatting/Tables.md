# Tables

## A table

A header row, a row of dashes, then the rows; `|` between the cells:

| Planet  | Moons | Ring |
|---------|-------|------|
| Earth   | 1     | no   |
| Jupiter | 95    | yes  |
| Saturn  | 146   | yes  |

The columns don't have to line up in the text; they're lined up when
shown.

## Alignment

A colon in the dash row aligns its column: `:---` left, `:---:` centered,
`---:` right.

| Left | Center | Right |
|:-----|:------:|------:|
| a    |   b    |     1 |
| left |  mid   |    10 |
| x    |   y    |   100 |
