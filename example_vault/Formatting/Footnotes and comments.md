# Footnotes and comments

## Footnotes

A footnote reference[^first] points to a note at the end of the page; the
label can be a word[^why]. An inline footnote^[written right in the text]
needs no definition.

"Footnotes" in the palette (**Ctrl+P**) lists them, and goes to one.

[^first]: The definition: `[^first]:` and its text.
[^why]: Labels are only names; they're shown in the order they're written.
