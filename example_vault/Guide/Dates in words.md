# Dates in words

Type `@` and a date in words on a new line below: a list of dates comes
up. Enter puts in a link to that day's note; Shift+Enter keeps your words
as the link's alias.

- `@today`, `@tomorrow`, `@yesterday`
- `@next friday`, `@last monday`, `@next week`, `@next month`
- `@in 3 days`, `@2 weeks ago`, `@in a month`
- `@oct 20`, `@20th of december`, `@end of month`, `@mid november`
- `@time` for times: `@time:now`, `@time:+1 hour`

Select words like *the day after tomorrow* and run "Parse natural language
date" (Ctrl+P) to turn them into a link; "Date picker" asks for one.
Settings → Dates: the date format, the trigger, links or plain text.
