# Citations

Cite the sources in a bibliography, and keep a **literature note** for each.
This vault's bibliography is [[refs.bib]] (a BibTeX file; a Zotero export
works as it is, BibTeX or CSL JSON).

## Citing

- "Citations: Insert citation" (Ctrl+P): choose a source; `[@ahrens2017]`
  goes in at the cursor.
- In the live preview a citation shows as its author and year, like these:
  [@luhmann1981] and [@ahrens2017, p. 12]. Several in one:
  [@ahrens2017; @clear2018]. Put the cursor on one to see what's written.
- Try it below: run "Insert citation" and type `zettel`.

## Literature notes

- "Citations: Open literature note": the note of the citation under the
  cursor (or a source you choose). The first time, it's made in
  `Reading notes/` from [[Literature note]]: title, authors, year, abstract,
  link. Put the cursor on `[@ahrens2017]` above and try it.
- "Citations: Insert link to literature note": `[[@ahrens2017]]`.

## Settings

Settings → Citations: the bibliography (in the vault, or an absolute path:
your Zotero export), the literature notes' folder, their name
(`@{{citekey}}`), their template (fields: `{{title}}`, `{{authors}}`,
`{{year}}`, `{{citekey}}`, `{{abstract}}`, `{{url}}`) and what "Insert
citation" writes (`[@{{citekey}}]`).
